How to Embed a YouTube Video into your Website




  1. Step 1

    The first step is to find the video that you want to embed. Any video will work, although YouTube Shorts will not.

  2. Step 2

    The second step is to click on the 'share' button under the video.

  3. Step 3

    The third step is to click on the 'embed' option in the menu that appears.

  4. Step 4

    The fourth step is to then configure the settings allowed in the embedding menu, and then press copy at the bottom of the menu.

  5. Step 5

    Paste your generated HTML iframe tag into your code and style it as you please.
Find out more about Embedding at https://www.w3schools.com/
