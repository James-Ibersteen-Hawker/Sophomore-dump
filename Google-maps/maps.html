How to Embed a Google Map into your Website




  1. Step 1

    The first step is to locate the place on the map, and open the map sidebar.

  2. Step 2

    The second step is to click on the 'share' button on the sidebar.

  3. Step 3

    The third step is to click on the embed-a-map button. Then, press 'Copy HTML'.

  4. Step 4

    The last step is to paste the copied code into your website.
Find out more about Embedding at https://www.w3schools.com/
