About Me

Born in Brooklyn and raised in Marlboro, Remy Serbinenko is a sophomore at Marlboro High School.

He is currently learning web development, and means business when it comes to creating. Remy knows the basics of HTML, CSS, and is looking forward to learning Javascript. He also has experience creating 3D models and renders in Blender and Autodesk Maya, and has studied animation at the School of Visual Arts at the end of his freshman year.

With this knowledge Remy is capable of creating websites with stunning color, beautiful graphics, and well-chosen style.

When he is not busy creating, Remy enjoys playing the piano, photography, and writing short stories.
