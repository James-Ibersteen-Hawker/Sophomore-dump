- The History of the Archive -
Constructed by Remy, Alternate Aircraft Archive was created to house the many ideas that Remy had. These ideas were mostly of aircraft, from wild and wacky designs to ones that are almost practical and can be built. Today the archive still remains unfinished, though its collection will grow with time.
