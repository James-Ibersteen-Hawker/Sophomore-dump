Things to Do

What to do in Tromsø





Tromsø Troll Museum

The Tromsø Troll Museum, situated less than 0.62 miles from city center, is a museum dedicated to preserving and displaying Norse troll fairy tales. Through its unique blend of real models and virtual/augmented reality, the Troll Museum is unlike any other.

Kaigata 3, 9008 Tromsø, Norway
+47 94 07 33 57
info@trollmuseum.no
https://trollmuseum.no/




Tromsø Fjords

As Tromsø is located on the island of Tromsøya, approximately 1.5 miles away from Hungeren, the nearest city on the neighboring island, around Tromsø can be seen a spectacular array of mountain fjords.

Fredrik Langesgate 2, 9008 Tromsø, Norway
+47 77 66 64 01
no email available
https://www.scandichotels.no/hotell/norge/tromso/scandic-ishavshotel




Tromsø Aurora Borealis

Tromsø is perhaps most famous for the frequency of aurora borealis sightings. This is because Tromsø is located less than 217 miles north of the Artic Circle. The magic of the northern lights has attracted visitors for decades.

Grønnegata 48, 9008 Tromsø, Norway
+47 77 78 10 50
viking@entertromso.no
https://entertromso.no/hjem-2/
