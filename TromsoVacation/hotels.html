Hotels

Experience the hotels of Tromsø





Tromsø Lodge and Camping

The Tromsø Lodge and Camping Ground is a camping ground across the fjord from Tromsø itself, and in addition to campgrounds it also has comfortable lodges for guests to stay in and relax.

Arthur Arntzens Veg 10, 9020 Tromsdalen, Norway
+47 77 63 80 37
post@tromsocamping.no
https://tromsolodgeandcamping.no/




Scandic Ishavshotel Hotel

Sometimes called the 'Paris of the North', the Scandic Ishavshotel is positioned on the edge of the Tromsø bay, giving its guests a spectacular view of the water as well as the Norwegian mountains.

Fredrik Langesgate 2, 9008 Tromsø, Norway
+47 77 66 64 01
no email available
https://www.scandichotels.no/hotell/norge/tromso/scandic-ishavshotel




Enter Tromsø City Hotel

The Enter City Hotel is a large hotel positioned right in the center of town, with over 46 rooms and apartments. It was recently remodelled and not has a fresh and modern feel.

Grønnegata 48, 9008 Tromsø, Norway
+47 77 78 10 50
viking@entertromso.no
https://entertromso.no/hjem-2/
