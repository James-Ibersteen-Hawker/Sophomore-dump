Tromsø Airport

The Tromsø Airport is situated on the northern end of the island off of Langnes. It is owned by Avinor, and receives regular flights from airlines including Lufthansa, Finnair, Air France, and Austrian Airlines.

City buses run within the city of Tromsø from early morning to late night, every day. Tickets may be purchased from ticket machines, sale points or in the app Troms Billett.

There is a daily train service from Oslo, Bergen and Trondheim to Fauske, from where there is a long-distance bus to Tromsø via Narvik. There are no trains that go directly to Tromsø.
