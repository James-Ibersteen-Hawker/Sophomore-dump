Restaurants

Experience the Restaurants of Tromsø





Bardus Bistro & Bar

The Bardus Bistro is a northern reflection of a traditional bistro, with a menu to suit, crafted from local ingredients including moose, reindeer, king crab, and fresh fish, alongside many other traditional, fresh, and locally available ingredients.

Cora Sandelsgate 4, 9008 Tromsø, Norway
+47 92 67 48 88
post@bardus.no
https://bardus.no/




Art Café Bistro

The Art Café Bistro is a quaint restaurant not far from the center of the city, and offers both lunch and breakfast under reservation. Its dishes are made from the local ingredients, and embody the taste of Tromsø.

Richard Withsplass 2, 9008 Tromsø, Norway
+47 93 98 04 75
unavailable
https://www.facebook.com/ArtCafeTromso/




Hildr Gastro Bar

Hildr focuses on both internationally and locally inspired dishes, tasty cocktails, and carefully selected wines and beers. The menu is constantly changing, based on both season and the chef’s mood. Hildr strives to create fun dishes that surprise and inspire.

Skippergata 11, 9008 Tromsø, Norway
+47 94 09 62 47
post@hildr.no
https://www.hildr.no/
