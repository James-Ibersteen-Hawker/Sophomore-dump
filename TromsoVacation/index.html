History of Tromsø

Tromsø is located 217.5 miles north of the Arctic Circle, or 1367 miles south of the North Pole, and is the fifth most populous city in Norway. Tromsø was established in 1250 and was formalized as a town in 1794 by King Christian VII. By the end of the 19th century, it had become a major Arctic trade center from which many Arctic expeditions originated. During World War II it served briefly as the seat of the Norwegian government.

Tromsø at Night

Tromsø in the nighttime is the perfect time to see the aurora borealis.
