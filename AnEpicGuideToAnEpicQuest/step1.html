Step 1:
Build Your Party

The first step before going on an Epic Quest is to find out who is in your party. You must select based on strengths first, rather than on emotion. A good choice is typically either a knight, but often any other strong unit fits the job.

Knight

Clad in armor from head to toe, a knight is good against a mighty foe.

 Barbarian

Raging Berserkers, in the frenzy of the fight. They will hit anything, regardless if it's right.

 Archer

Silent and deadly, swift and stealthy, the arrow sings through the air.

 Blacksmith

Hammer in hand, can fix and repair, the tools of adventurers, to face the dangers they dare.

 Hunter

Eagle in hand, a sword at his side. A hunter knows how his time to bide.

 Commander

A leader to lead, and control the might. It is he who determines who wins or who loses the fight.
