- On Epic Quests -
When going on an Epic Quest there are several things to keep in mind. The first thing to know is in what context are you performing your Epic Quest. The second main thing is to be aware of who you are going to face on your Epic Quest. And lastly, and MOST importantly, it is absolutely crucial to know what the reward for your Epic Quest will be. Preferrably, in gold.
