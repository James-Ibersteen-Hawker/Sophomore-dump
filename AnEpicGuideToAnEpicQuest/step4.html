Step 4:
Determine if Your Reward is Suitable for the Enemies You Will Face

Here at Epic Quests we make sure that you are more than compensated for the expenses of your Epic Quests. With that thought in mind, we would like to stress that an Epic Quest is not worth it without an Epic Reward. Thus, below we have provided a table which can help you approximate if the reward you are being offered is enough for you to risk yourself to undertake an Epic Quest. The table is meant to be a helpful guide, and if you are lucky enough to be offered a reward greater than the reward conversion listed, then that's Epic!
